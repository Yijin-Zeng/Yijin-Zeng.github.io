Hello, I'm Yijin Zeng.

I'm a final year PhD candidate in Statistics and Machine Learning at Imperial College London, jointly with the University of Oxford through the StatML CDT.

My research focuses on developing new methodologies for statistical hypothesis testing, correlation analysis, and change point detection in the presence of missing data — with applications in A/B testing, causal inference, information retrieval, and continual learning.
