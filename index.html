Hi, I'm Yijin Zeng (曾一津).

I'm a machine learning scientist at Expedia Group, working on Expedia's B2B Revenue Optimisation & Data Science Team. Prior to this, I completed my PhD in Statistics and Machine Learning at Imperial College London, jointly with the University of Oxford through the StatML CDT program.

My PhD research focused on developing rigorous statistical methodologies for hypothesis testing, correlation analysis, and change-point detection in the presence of missing data. You can find my publications in statistics and machine learning journals.

At Expedia, I enjoy taking ownership of end-to-end machine learning pipelines. Outside of work, I love diving deep into different topics such as LLMs, computer science, and mathematics. I invite you to explore my notes, including my handwritten PDFs, as well as various hands-on projects I've built for fun. I'm also passionate about sharing knowledge through engagements such as tech talks, teaching, and paper reading sessions.

Papers

Exact Bounds of Spearman's Footrule in the Presence of Missing Data with Applications to Independence Testing
Yijin Zeng, Niall M. Adams, Dean A. Bodenham
Electron. J. Statist. 19(2): 6103-6140 (2025) · 2025
This work derives mathematical tight bounds for Spearman's footrule (a robust alternative to Spearman's rank correlation) in the presence of missing data, proposed efficient algorithms for computing the bounds, and applies the bounds independence testing with missing data.
Electronic Journal of Statistics (EJS)
MMD Two-Sample Testing in the Presence of Arbitrarily Missing Data
Yijin Zeng, Niall M. Adams, Dean A. Bodenham
Transactions on Machine Learning Research (11/2025) · 2024
This work extends the popular maximum mean discrepancy (MMD) two-sample testing methods to the cases where data might be missing or partially observed.
Transactions on Machine Learning Research (TMLR)

Yijin Zeng, Niall M. Adams, Dean A. Bodenham
